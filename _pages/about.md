About
======
I'm a master's student in Medialogy at Aalborg University Copenhagen, where I also earned my BSc. I work on immersive technologies, embodied interaction, and physiological computing. More recently I have turned to the phenomenology of immersive media, asking how virtual environments shape first-person lived experience, with a current focus on embodiment and self-representation in VR. I am in my 9th semester, beginning an internship at the Augmented Cognition Lab (AAU Copenhagen).
 
I am a student assistant at the Creative Technology Lab (AAU CREATE), and was an XR Research Assistant on the Balance VR project at the Multisensory Experience Lab, working on balance and visually induced motion sickness in VR. That work grew out of our BSc project.
